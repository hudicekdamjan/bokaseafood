Our story

Where the mountains
meet the sea.

Boka Seafood began at sea, not in a kitchen. We farm mussels and oysters on long lines in the Bay of Kotor, where cold mountain springs meet the Adriatic and give our shellfish their clean, sweet taste.

The restaurant came later, so guests could eat them the way we do: straight from the sea, simply prepared, with a glass of our home-made wine and the whole bay in front of you.

Family experience

Captain Dražen,
age 10.

Meet the youngest captain in Boka! Our brother Dražen is ten, has his own small boat and a huge love for our farm. When the sea is calm and clear, he will take you out to the farm to see the rows of mussels and oysters beneath the surface. Along the way he will tell you everything he knows about shellfish, sometimes by the book and sometimes in his very own version. We can’t promise the most expert talk in Boka, but we can guarantee the most charming one.

  1. 01
    Board with the captain

    A small boat, two passengers and one very serious captain.

  2. 02
    Rows beneath the sea

    When the water is clear, the shellfish ropes are right there below you.

  3. 03
    Dražen’s lecture

    Everything he knows about mussels and oysters, plus something new every time.

  4. 04
