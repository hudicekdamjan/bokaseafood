Our story

Where the mountains
meet the sea.

Boka Seafood began at sea, not in a kitchen. We farm mussels and oysters on long lines in the Bay of Kotor, where cold mountain springs meet the Adriatic and give our shellfish their clean, sweet taste.

The restaurant came later, so guests could eat them the way we do: straight from the sea, simply prepared, with a glass of local wine and the whole bay in front of you.
